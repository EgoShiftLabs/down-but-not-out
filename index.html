Who is this community for?

Adults 18 and older with and without disabilities who are interested in genuine, consensual romantic connections. Adults with Down syndrome are part of the community we want to serve. Everyone deserves to be approached as an individual.

Can I create a profile yet?

Not yet. This is our introductory page. Member profiles, matching, and messaging are not available, and we are not accepting registrations here.

What are you planning for accessibility?

Our priorities include clear language, readable screens, keyboard access, and flexible ways to communicate. We want adults with disabilities involved in shaping the experience as it develops.

What about privacy and safety?

This page has no signup form, advertising trackers, or personal-data collection by us. GitHub Pages hosts the page and may process technical connection information. Before member features launch, the community will need clear privacy practices, consent-centered design, reporting and blocking, and moderation.

Is this an agency or a support service?

No. This project is being developed as a dating community. It is not a care service, matchmaking agency, or substitute for individual support.
