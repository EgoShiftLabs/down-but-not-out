A few things to know

Fair questions.
Straight answers.

Something real takes care to build.
Here’s where we are.

Can I join or meet someone here yet?+

Not yet. This is a live introduction and interactive product preview. There are no real member profiles, accounts, matching, or private messages. The profile studio lets you explore the idea without signing up.

Who is Down but not Out for?+

Adults 18 and older with and without disabilities who want genuine, consensual romantic connections. Adults with Down syndrome are part of the community we want to serve. Each person’s preferences, individuality, and choices come first.

Is the person in the campaign a member?+

No. The campaign features a fictional, AI-generated adult model. The image represents our creative direction. It is not a member profile or testimonial.

What happens to my profile preview?+

By default, your preview exists only while the page is open and disappears on refresh. You can optionally choose “Remember my preview on this device” to save it only in this browser’s local storage; it is not uploaded or shared with us. Avoid sensitive personal information, especially on shared devices. Turning the option off or selecting Reset removes the saved preview.

Can I help fund development?+

Yes. Our independent GoFundMe campaign supports the next stage of the platform, including accessibility, safety, and early beta development. This is a commercial startup project, not a nonprofit charity.

Is the $DBNO token required to join?+

No. $DBNO is a separate speculative meme token. There is no token requirement to access the future dating platform, and token purchases are not donations. Our official development fundraiser is linked in the support section.

How can I keep up with the project?+

Use the Request email updates link to open your email app and send our team a message, or bookmark the public roadmap on this page. This is a manual email request, not an automated signup or confirmed place on a waitlist.

What about privacy and accessibility?+

This website has no advertising trackers or embedded signup collection. GitHub Pages, our host, may process connection information. The text-size and high-contrast choices are saved only in your browser; sample profile entries are not sent and are saved locally only if you opt in. Contacting us by email shares what you choose to send with our team and your email provider. Please do not include diagnoses or other sensitive details in feedback messages. Full member privacy and moderation processes are needed before dating features open.
